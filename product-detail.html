Product Description

JADA POHA (900GM PACK): Jada Poha is a type of flattened rice commonly used in Indian cuisine. It is a variety of flattened rice that has thicker flakes compared to regular poha. Jada Poha is typically used to make a popular Indian breakfast dish called Aloo Poha, which consists of cooked poha mixed with potatoes, spices, and herbs.
A delicious and satisfying Indian dish made from flattened rice flakes and potatoes.
This flavorful combination creates a light and tasty breakfast or snack that is loved by many.
