About us

Lotan Groups has established itself as a trusted name among consumers and businesses alike. The company specializes in the production of poha, a popular Indian breakfast dish made from flattened rice. Lotan Groups takes pride in its state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities that adhere to stringent quality control measures, ensuring that every batch of poha is of the highest standards. From sourcing the best-quality rice grains to employing advanced processing techniques, Lotan Groups guarantees that their poha is not only delicious but also retains its nutritional value, texture, and freshness.

At Lotan Groups, their dedication to excellence extends beyond the manufacturing process. They have built a robust supply chain network, allowing them to procure the finest rice grains from trusted farmers and suppliers. By maintaining strong relationships with their partners, Lotan Groups ensures a steady supply of high-quality rice, which forms the foundation of their poha production. Furthermore, the company prioritizes sustainability and eco-friendly practices throughout their operations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality, nutritious, and flavorful poha and rice products to our customers while promoting sustainable agriculture practices and supporting local farmers.
We aim to be a trusted brand known for our commitment to excellence, integrity, and customer satisfaction.
Through continuous innovation, efficient manufacturing processes, and a focus on food safety, we strive to contribute to the well-being and happiness of our consumers and the communities we serve.

Our Vision

Our vision is to be the leading poha and rice manufacturing company, recognized globally for our premium products and commitment to sustainable practices.
We aim to create a positive impact on the food industry by consistently delivering innovative, healthy, and authentic offerings that cater to diverse consumer preferences. Through continuous growth, strong partnerships, and a dedication to excellence, we strive to contribute to a world where nutritious and delicious food is accessible to all.
