Our heritage

Generations of Pure Milling & Roasting Expertise

Founded in 1964 as a small family-run processing mill, Lotan Groups has spent six decades perfecting the delicate balance between ancient harvesting wisdom and contemporary sorting purity.

We believe good food starts with respect for the soil. Every grain of basmati, every flake of poha, and every crisp snack in our portfolio is meticulously selected, slow-conditioned, and roasted under strict family supervision. No shortcuts. Just clean, natural nourishment.
