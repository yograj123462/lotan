How to cook Poha

  • 1 cup poha (flattened rice)
  • 1 small onion finely chopped
  • 1 small tomato finely chopped
  • 1 green chili finely chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 8–10 curry leaves
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped (for garnish)
  • Lemon wedges (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Place the poha in a colander and rinse it under running water for a few seconds. Drain the water and let the poha sit for 1–2 minutes to soften.
  2. Check the texture of the poha. It should be soft and easily breakable between your fingers. If still hard, sprinkle a little water over it and let it sit for another minute or two.
  3. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Add mustard seeds and cumin seeds. Let them crackle.
  4. Add curry leaves and green chili. Sauté for a few seconds until they release their aroma.
  5. Add chopped onions and sauté until translucent.
  6. Add the finely chopped tomato and cook until it becomes soft.
  7. Sprinkle turmeric powder and salt over the mixture. Mix well.
  8. Gently fluff the soaked poha with a fork and add it to the pan. Mix everything together.
  9. Cook for 2–3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the poha is heated through.
  10. Remove from heat and garnish with freshly chopped coriander leaves.
Serve hot with a side of lemon wedges for an extra tangy flavour, if desired.

Note: Poha is a quick-cooking ingredient. Follow the soaking time for your chosen variety to achieve the desired texture.
